Weekly Team Reports

Week 1

Date: 8/31/2026

This week, we got together and prepared for the RaiderTrainer project. First, we held a virtual meeting and introduced each other to break the ice. After that, a shared calendar was created for future group meetings and a GitHub repository was made for the team to upload to for the project. Then, we had discussions on splitting up workload and overall group roles for future weeks and overall background research on the project. Next, we committed to slack as our main communication method and created new channels for organized messaging. Finally, we looked over the content for requirements and the project website in preparation for future development.

Week 2

Date: 9/7/2026

This week we had two virtual meetings with our team, on Tuesday and Thursday. The first meeting we discussed the projects minimum requirements that have to be approved by the upcoming Monday. This allowed us time to prepare for our next meeting where we compared our different requirement pages. After finishing those, we spent some time on our own to do more research into different parts of the project. For example, looking into GitHub pages for future website use, provided starter code and future needed python libraries, and OCR using EasyOCR.

Seniya worked on AprilTag detection portion of the RaiderTrainer project. Joe created python code for miscellaneous parts of the project, like rep counting and angle detection.
